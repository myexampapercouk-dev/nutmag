The cost of an AI chatbot depends far more on what it has to do than on the AI itself. A bot that only answers FAQs can run on a low monthly subscription. A custom AI assistant that reads your live stock, takes bookings and updates your CRM is a one-time build plus small monthly running costs.

Here is what actually drives the price, so you can tell which kind you need before you talk to anyone.

## Off-the-shelf chatbot or custom AI assistant?

There are two very different products sold under the word “chatbot”.

|  | Off-the-shelf chatbot tool | Custom AI assistant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How you pay | Monthly subscription | One-time build, then optional monthly care |
| Setup | You configure it yourself | Built around your business for you |
| What it knows | FAQs and pages you upload | Your live data: products, stock, prices, bookings, orders |
| What it can do | Answer common questions | Answer, check, book, update systems and hand off to staff |
| Best for | Simple websites with a few repeat questions | Businesses where questions depend on real-time data |

If most of your customer questions are the same ten, a subscription tool is often enough. If customers ask things like “do you have this in stock, in my size, under my budget?”, you need something connected to your systems.

## The five things that set the price of a custom AI assistant

1. **Integrations.** Each system the assistant connects to — Shopify, a booking tool, a CRM, WhatsApp — adds work. This is usually the biggest cost driver.
2. **Actions, not just answers.** Reading information is simpler than doing things: creating bookings, updating orders, sending documents or taking payments.
3. **Your business rules.** Pricing logic, approval limits, when to hand a customer to a person. The more rules, the more building and testing.
4. **Channels.** Website chat only, or also WhatsApp, email and a staff dashboard.
5. **Testing on your real cases.** A good build is tested against real past conversations before launch. It takes time, and it is what makes the assistant reliable.

## What it costs to run each month

After launch you pay for three things:

- **AI usage:** the AI model charges per conversation. For most small businesses this is small compared with staff time saved.
- **Hosting:** where the assistant runs.
- **Care and improvements (optional):** monitoring, fixes and monthly tuning. You can also take over and run it yourself.

## How to get an accurate price without guesswork

Hourly estimates for AI work are hard to trust, because the scope is unclear until someone looks at your actual process. A better approach:

1. Describe one process that costs you the most time.
2. Ask for a working demo on a real example from your business.
3. Get a fixed quote based on what you saw working.

You know exactly what you are paying for, and you have seen it work before you commit.

## Is it worth it?

A quick check: estimate how many hours a week your team spends answering repeat questions or doing the task by hand, multiply by what that time costs, and compare it with the build cost. If the assistant pays for itself within a few months, it is usually worth doing.

## FAQ

**Can I start with a simple chatbot and upgrade later?** Yes. Many businesses start with answers only, then add actions like bookings or order lookups once they see what customers ask most.

**Do I need technical staff to run an AI chatbot?** No. A custom build should come with setup, hosting and training. Your team only needs to handle the conversations it hands over.

**Will the chatbot give wrong answers?** A well-built assistant answers only from your data and rules, and hands anything uncertain to a person. Testing on real past cases before launch is what keeps errors rare.

**How long does it take to build?** A focused assistant connected to one or two systems typically goes live in a few weeks after the demo is approved.
